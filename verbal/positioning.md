# Positioning

- **Company:** Antigen
- **Category:** AI adversary / autonomous penetration testing for enterprise security.
- **What it does:** Deploys AI pen-testing agents against production systems every night and pairs them with a monthly human red-team assessment. Every finding ships with traces, reproduction steps, and remediation — delivered directly in GitHub, Linear, or the Antigen Platform.
- **Who it serves:** Enterprise security teams defending production systems and their full attack surface.
- **The one-line pitch:** *Accelerating enterprise defense at agentspeed.*
- **Web / Contact:** antigen.sh
