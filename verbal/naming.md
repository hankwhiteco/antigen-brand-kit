# Naming

## Master positioning line
Use often, near the logo.

> **Secures your entire attack surface**

## Product & mark naming
Antigen is a single product, no divisions or sub-brands.

- **Antigen**, the company and the product. Domain: **antigen.sh**.
- **Antigen Platform**, the product surface where findings are delivered (alongside GitHub and Linear).
- **Email pattern:** `name@antigen.sh` (e.g. `alex@antigen.sh`).

## Signature marks & motifs
- **Eyebrow / master line:** `SECURES YOUR ENTIRE ATTACK SURFACE`, Chivo Mono, uppercase, tracked, Scarlet.
- **Coined term:** *agentspeed*, lowercase, one word.
- **Number motif:** `02.16` / `0216`, a recurring edition/marker number set in Chivo Mono, usually Scarlet or reversed. Treat it as a brand device, not literal data.
