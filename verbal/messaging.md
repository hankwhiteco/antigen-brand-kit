# Voice & messaging

**Tone:** Technical, precise, and adversarial-but-trusted. Antigen speaks to security engineers as a peer who has already broken in, confident, unhype, evidence-led. Sentence-case headlines carry the weight; mono labels do the shouting.

The master positioning line and product naming live in [`naming.md`](naming.md).

## Writing rules
- Headlines are **sentence case**, not all-caps, set in Clash Display, tight. Caps is reserved for mono eyebrows, labels, and CTAs.
- Coin precise compounds when they earn it, *"agentspeed."* Don't pad with generic security-vendor filler.
- Lead with the mechanism and the outcome: what the agent does (maps, exploits, secures) and what the customer gets (traces, repro steps, remediation).
- Keep the delivery aside tight with a comma, not an em dash. e.g. *"…remediation, delivered directly in Github, Linear, or the Antigen Platform."*
- Eyebrows in Chivo Mono, uppercase, tracked, Scarlet, e.g. `SECURES YOUR ENTIRE ATTACK SURFACE`.
