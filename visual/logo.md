# Logo

The mark is a radial burst ("antigen") icon. The primary lockup is the **icon + "Antigen" wordmark**. Files live in [`assets/logos/`](assets/logos/), **SVG** for screen/scalable use, **PNG** for raster. Every variant ships in Dark (Bark), White, Red (Scarlet), Grey (Greige), and Sage as applicable, with `on White` / `on Dark` / `on Grey` context versions.

## Files
| File | When to use |
|---|---|
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Logo – Dark.svg` · `Antigen Logo – Dark on White.svg` | **Primary lockup** on light grounds (White, Rice, Greige). |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Logo – White.svg` | **Reversed** lockup on Bark / dark sections. |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Logo – Red on Dark.svg` | Lockup on Bark when a Scarlet accent is wanted. |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Wordmark – Dark.svg` · `White.svg` · `Dark on White.svg` · `White on Dark.svg` | **Wordmark only**, when the mark is already present or space is tight. |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Icon – Dark.svg` · `White.svg` · `Red.svg` · `Dark on White.svg` · `Dark on Grey.svg` · `Red on Dark.svg` | **Mark / default**, app/favicon, inline marker, tight spaces. |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Icon Thin – …` (Dark · White · Red · Sage · on White/Grey · Red on Dark) | **Mark / thin**, section markers and oversized ghost/watermark treatments. |
| `assets/logos/svg/Antigen Avatar – Dark.svg` · `White.svg` · `Grey.svg` · `Dark + Red.svg` | Social avatars / app badge. |
| `assets/logos/png/…` | PNG equivalents of every variant above. |

## Color selection rules
- **On light backgrounds** (White / Rice / Greige): Dark (Bark) lockup.
- **On Bark / dark sections:** White (Reversed) lockup.
- **On Scarlet:** White lockup only, never the Dark mark.
- **Mark color:** default Bark; use White or Scarlet where contrast or accent requires; Sage only for the thin mark as a graphic.

## Clear space & sizing
- **Clear space:** keep a clear margin around the logo at least equal to **1× the icon height** on all sides, nothing intrudes into that zone.
- **Minimum size:** keep the wordmark legible; below that, switch to the icon (mark) alone.

## Don'ts
- Don't recolor outside the approved variants, add effects/shadows, stretch, or rotate the logo.
- Don't put the Dark lockup on Bark or Scarlet (use White).
- Don't crowd the clear-space zone.
